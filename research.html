Parkinson's Disease — Voice
Speech biomarkers in rapid eye movement sleep behavior disorder and Parkinson disease
Rusz et al. · Annals of Neurology · 2021
+
Key finding
7-center international study. Monopitch identified as the single most specific prodromal Parkinson's marker — measurable up to 12 months before motor symptoms appear.
How DreamTrack uses it
Sets the f0_std < 20 Hz threshold. If pitch variation drops below this level, DreamTrack adds +0.30 to the Parkinson's score — the highest-weighted single feature.
doi.org/10.1002/ana.26085 ↗
From prodromal stages to clinical trials: The promise of digital speech biomarkers in Parkinson's disease
Rusz, Krack, Tripoliti · Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews · 2024
+
Key finding
One year of longitudinal speech monitoring is sufficient to detect Parkinson's progression. Loudness variation and articulation clarity are among the most sensitive markers.
How DreamTrack uses it
Sets the energy flatness threshold (energy_std_db < 4). Low loudness variation adds +0.20 to the Parkinson's score.
doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2024.105922 ↗
Vocal feature changes for monitoring Parkinson's disease progression: a systematic review

+
Key finding

How DreamTrack uses it
Validates the feature set DreamTrack extracts via librosa: jitter, shimmer, HNR, speech rate, and pause ratio are all in the validated 18-feature set.
doi.org/10.3390/brainsci15030320 ↗
Clinical Measurement of Speech and Voice (2nd ed.)

Key finding
Study of 13,000+ individuals. APOE4 homozygosity proposed as a distinct genetic AD form: >95% penetrance, amyloid pathology near-universal by age 65. One copy (het) raises risk 3–5x with biomarker changes ~7 years earlier than non-carriers.
