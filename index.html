How it works

One recording. Two analyses.

Voice analysis
How you sound
The app measures 89 acoustic features — pitch variation, speech rate, pause patterns, vocal tremor. These are the same markers researchers use in clinical studies. Each feature maps to a specific condition with a citation from a peer-reviewed paper.
Dream analysis
What you say
Claude AI reads your dream narration and scores it for coherence, emotional tone, vocabulary complexity, and narrative structure. How you tell a story changes before your memory does.
Audio → Amazon Transcribe + Whisper → transcript → librosa (89 features) + Claude (dream NLP) → risk scores → stored in PostgreSQL
Features

What the app gives you

Trend charts
30 / 90 / 180 days
Your scores over time. Mann-Kendall statistical test flags real trends vs. normal variation. One data point means nothing — the pattern over months does.
GP Report
A PDF for your doctor
Color-coded table — green (normal), amber (watch), red (elevated). Every row has a citation. You can hand it to a doctor who has never heard of DreamTrack.
Dream video
AI film of your dream
After analysis, you can generate a short video of your dream with one tap. Claude writes the visual scene, Runway Gen-4 Turbo renders it. Stored privately in S3.
Genetic personalisation
APOE ε4, LRRK2, GBA1, BAP1
Enter known mutations and your alert thresholds adjust. Someone with APOE ε4 needs a different baseline than someone without.
Research page
30+ peer-reviewed papers
Every score has a source. The research page lists all the studies the scoring system is built on — so you can read the original papers yourself.

Analysis · REM Sleep Behavior Disorder

How the RBD dream score is built

Two-layer analysis: rule-based keyword detection → Claude semantic override. Claude wins if available.

Layer 1 — Keyword rules (Python)
Human attack words AND animal attack words both present
+0.40
Human OR animal attack (one type only)
+0.25
First-person motion verbs (run, flee, fight…) ≥ 2
≤+0.25
nightmare_indicator (valence < −0.3 AND arousal > 0.4 AND motion)
+0.20
emotional_arousal > 0.5
+0.15

Human attacks: chased, grabbed, attacked, strangled… Animal attacks: wolf, bear, lion, bitten, mauled… (only unambiguously threatening — generic "dog" excluded)

Layer 2 — Claude semantic scoring (overrides Layer 1)

Claude reads the full transcript and scores on the same 0–1 scale. It handles negation ("I was not being chased"), context ("the monster was friendly"), and ambiguous language that keyword rules miss.

Grounding in the prompt:
"Being chased or attacked by humans is the most diagnostically specific RBD marker (76.8% of RBD patients — Fasiello 2022). Animal attacks: 39.9%."

What I learned building this

The hard parts.

01
Reading 30+ research papers
I read the actual studies — not summaries. Every score threshold (like "F0 std < 20 Hz for Parkinson's") came from a specific paper. If I couldn't find a citation, I didn't add the feature.
02
Voice analysis is harder than it looks
Recording quality, background noise, language, and recording length all affect the signal. I used librosa to extract 89 acoustic features and mapped each one to a condition with a threshold.
03
Responsible design
This is a wellness tool, not a medical device. I spent a lot of time on the disclaimer language, the GP report design, and making sure nothing in the app looks like a diagnosis.
04
Building alone is slow — but you learn everything
I built the frontend, backend, ML pipeline, database schema, deployment, and iOS app myself. It took longer but I understand every line of code.
What's next

This is a starting point.

The app is live and collecting real data. The next step is research — turning this tool into a validated study.

Research question
Can a 60-second recording detect early signs of neurological change?
The literature says yes. But none of those studies used a consumer mobile app. DreamTrack is the first attempt to bring this into everyday life.
Next steps

Working toward a collaboration with a neuroscience lab to run a longitudinal pilot study using DreamTrack as the data collection platform.
