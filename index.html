A unique feature

Every dream becomes
a short film.

After the analysis is done, you tap one button and the app generates a unique AI video of your dream. It uses Runway Gen-4 Turbo. The video is generated on demand — nothing happens automatically. It's stored privately in S3.

Step 1
Claude writes the scene
Claude reads the dream transcript and writes a cinematic 50-word visual prompt. If you have a profile photo, Claude Vision describes your appearance and weaves it into the scene.
Step 2
Runway renders the video
The prompt goes to Runway Gen-4 Turbo image-to-video. The backend polls until the task finishes, then downloads the result and stores it permanently in S3.
Step 3
Moderation handled gracefully
If Runway blocks the prompt, Claude automatically rewrites it softer and retries. If it's still blocked, a quiet message appears instead of a broken error screen.

Statistical foundation

Mann-Kendall: detecting real trends, not random noise.

A bad week looks like a trend. A real trend looks like noise. Mann-Kendall separates the two — and DreamTrack will not fire an alert unless three independent gates all pass at the same time.

What the p-value means (simply)
"How likely is this drift just random chance?" A p-value of 0.03 means only a 3% probability the worsening is a coincidence. DreamTrack requires p < 0.05 before any alert fires.
The S statistic — how it works
For every pair of observations (xᵢ, xₒ) where i < j:
  If score goes up: S += 1
  If score goes down: S -= 1
  If equal: S += 0
A large positive S means consistently worsening. Sen's slope gives the rate of change per day.
Why Mann-Kendall and not linear regression?
Non-parametric — no assumption that data is normally distributed.
Outlier-robust — one spike day does not corrupt the entire trend estimate.
Monotone-sensitive — detects consistent directional change even when the rate varies.
Three gates — ALL must pass before an alert fires
1
Statistical: p < 0.05 and Sen's slope > 0.005 per day. The trend must be both statistically significant and large enough to matter.
2
Multi-modal: At least 2 independent conditions must be worsening at the same time. One biomarker drifting alone never triggers an alert.
3
Time minimum: 14 days = info, 30 days = warning, 60 days = concern. Short windows cannot fire the higher alert levels.
Two real examples
Single bad week
Scores spike for 5 days, then recover. S statistic is near zero — too many up/down pairs cancel out. Result: p = 0.43 → "stable". No alert.
Six weeks of consistent drift
Scores rise slightly but consistently across 42 days. S accumulates strongly positive. Result: p = 0.03 → "worsening". Alert fires if gate 2 also met.
From research to code

Every threshold comes from a published paper.

I didn't make up the numbers. Every scoring decision has an inline citation in the actual code.

VOICE
f0_std < 20 Hz scores 0.30 for Parkinson's
Monopitch is the only acoustic feature that distinguished prodromal iRBD from healthy controls across all 7 sites in a 12-month longitudinal follow-up. It's the single most specific prodromal Parkinson's marker — measurable 12 months before motor symptoms. Rusz et al., Annals of Neurology, 2021
VOICE
speech_rate < 2.5 syl/sec + pause > 800 ms for Alzheimer's
Speech rate and pause duration are the two strongest acoustic Alzheimer's markers according to a 2024 meta-analysis of 22 studies. Saeedi et al., Journal of Prevention of Alzheimer's Disease, 2024
VOICE
Pitch slope, kurtosis & skewness are the primary depression discriminators
Menne 2024 identified pitch features as primary (η² = 0.30–0.31); energy variation is secondary. AUC 0.93 — as good as clinical questionnaires. DreamTrack implements f0_std < 12 Hz as the operational threshold. Menne et al., BMC Psychiatry, 2024
DREAM
Separate word lists for human attack and animal attack
These two attack types are the most diagnostically specific RBD dream markers. Both present together doubles the signal weight. Fasiello et al., Journal of Clinical Medicine, 2022
DREAM
Causal connective density and type-token ratio for FTD
Three linguistic features from a remote app distinguished frontotemporal dementia with 84.8% accuracy — no clinic visit required. Vonk et al., Alzheimer's and Dementia, 2025

AI Stack
Claude + Transcribe + Runway
Claude for dream NLP and video prompts. Amazon Transcribe (primary) + OpenAI Whisper (fallback) for speech-to-text. Runway Gen-4 Turbo for dream videos.
Analysis
librosa + spaCy + Mann-Kendall
89 acoustic features extracted from raw audio. spaCy for linguistic analysis. Mann-Kendall for statistically significant trend detection.
